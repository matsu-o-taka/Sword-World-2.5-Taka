**⏩△Mimicry (Incomplete)**

Mimics the form of a Humanoid. The details of this form differ for each individual, and there is no specific model (it cannot imitate someone's exact appearance). In Humanoid form, the abilities of the Body section cannot be used.

**◯Daemon's Cuisine / 9 (16) / Fortitude / Negates**

Prepares and serves dishes that stimulate desire using various ingredients. Characters who eat food prepared with this ability must make a resistance check. If failed, their Agility or Dexterity immediately decreases by 1 point, and they begin to treat this dish (and the monster who created it) as their most precious possession. They will spare no effort to eat this meal again, even refusing other food in order to obtain meals created by this ability.

The effect of ability score decay accumulates one point with each meal consumed. When the total decay of any ability score reaches 12, or if any ability score becomes 0 due to this effect, that character becomes unable to perform any actions or movements of their own will. This effect is treated as Curse + Psychic (see CR II, p. 128). Using this ability decreases the Barraganbuk's HP by 10 points.

This ability requires 1 hour to use. One use can prepare meals for approximately 10 Humanoids. It can only be used once per day.

**◯Delicate Loot**

If the HP of the Head section falls to 0 or less and the HP of the Body section is 1 or higher, the loot of "Daemon's Seasoning" will be lost.

###### Body

**►Engulf and Dissolve / 9 (16) / Fortitude / Negates**

Engulfs an enemy within their body and dissolves them with internal corrosive fluids. One target character within "Range: Touch" takes 2d points of fixed damage and is engulfed. The engulfed target takes 2d points of fixed damage at the start of this monster's turn. Furthermore, the target becomes immobilized and cannot perform any Major Actions other than escaping from the body.

Escape follows the rules for [de-bonding](/rules:de-bonding) (see ML, p. 71). This ability cannot target characters with multiple sections. Also, if a character is already engulfed by this ability, it cannot be used on other characters. Releasing an already engulfed character voluntarily is possible as a Minor Action.

When this ability deals fixed damage to a target, the Head section's HP is restored by the same amount.

**►Twin Strike & Double Attack**

The Body can make two Vine attacks each round. After checking the result of the first attack, the monster can choose whether to make a second attack on the same target further or on a different target of their choice.
